Photo verification

Snap it. Ozzily figures out the rest.

Vision AI reads your plate and estimates protein in seconds, and reads your gym selfie or watch screen to confirm the session counted. No weighing, no searching a food database at 9pm.

Streaks that mean something

Momentum you can feel in your texts.

Hit your protein and your lifts, and Ozzily keeps count out loud. Miss a day and the streak bends instead of resetting to zero.

Side-effect support

For the days the medication wins.

Nausea, sluggish digestion, dehydration, the sudden 4pm exhaustion. Text Ozzily what's happening and get practical, gentle adjustments, plus a nudge to call your prescriber when that's the right answer.

The intensity dial

Text "chill" or "intense" anytime.

You set the temperature. Vacation week? Say chill and Ozzily backs off to one soft check-in. Training for something? Say intense and it gets specific and checks in more often.
